Privacy policy for Tab Glide — Tab Scroll & Reorder.

Effective date: October 22, 2026 · Version 1.0.7

Local use of tab information

Tab Glide reads open tab titles, addresses, identifiers, order, pinned status and the active tab to display and operate a horizontal tab bar in the current window. It reads browser window state to apply fullscreen preferences. This information is processed locally, is not stored as browsing history, and is not transmitted to the developer or any external server.

Settings and website access

Your preferences are saved in Microsoft Edge’s local extension storage on this device. Scroll positions are kept per window in Microsoft Edge’s in-memory extension session storage and clear when the browser or extension restarts. This temporary state contains only window identifiers and numeric scroll offsets. Website permission is optional and lets Tab Glide insert its bar into permitted HTTP/HTTPS pages. The bar is an isolated extension frame. The extension does not read website form fields or page text. Favicons are requested from Microsoft Edge’s local favicon service, not a third-party icon provider.

No tracking or sale

There is no analytics, advertising, remote code, account system, sale of data or third-party data sharing. Uninstalling the extension removes its locally stored preferences. You can revoke website access from the settings page or Microsoft Edge’s extension controls.

Limited use

Tab Glide uses information received through browser extension APIs only to provide its tab-bar features, in accordance with the Microsoft Edge Add-ons data-use policies, including the Limited Use requirements. Tab information is not used for advertising, sold, transferred to the developer, or made available for human review.

Payments and donations

All Tab Glide features are free. When configured, the optional support link opens PayPal in a new tab only after you click it. You choose whether to contribute and how much on PayPal. PayPal processes the payment under its own privacy policy and terms. Tab Glide does not collect or process payment information, check payment status, or send your tab information or preferences to PayPal. The developer may receive payment information made available by PayPal to the recipient, such as the payer’s name, email address and contribution amount.

Contact and support

For support or privacy questions, email tabglideapp@gmail.com. If you contact support, your email address and the information you choose to send are used to respond to your request. The extension does not automatically attach tab information to support emails.
